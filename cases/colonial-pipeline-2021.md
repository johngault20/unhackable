# Colonial Pipeline ransomware incident (2021)

## Incident summary

The FBI publicly attributed the ransomware incident affecting Colonial Pipeline to the DarkSide ransomware variant. The incident disrupted pipeline operations and prompted a broader response. This case is included to study resilience and recovery, not to reproduce ransomware activity.

**Primary source:** [FBI: Statement on Compromise of Colonial Pipeline Networks](https://www.fbi.gov/news/press-releases/statement-on-compromise-of-colonial-pipeline-networks)

## Defensive lesson

Ransomware incidents can affect availability and operations, not only confidentiality. Backups, tested recovery procedures, timely patching, strong authentication, and a plan for isolating affected devices are important layers. A public incident does not establish how a different victim was initially accessed; avoid repeating unverified claims about entry paths.

## Safe checks for a home user

- Keep the operating system, browser, and commonly used applications updated from trusted sources.
- Use unique passwords and multifactor authentication where available, especially for email and remote-access accounts.
- Keep backups of important files, and periodically confirm that needed files can be restored.
- Know how to disconnect your own affected device from a network and contact a trusted support professional if you suspect ransomware. Do not run unknown cleanup tools.

## Relevance to this project

This case supports basic prevention and recovery planning. It is not evidence that a local device is infected, and `unhackable` does not detect or remediate ransomware.
